FieldStation42 is installed.

GETTING STARTED

  1. Start FieldStation42 from the Start Menu. A video window opens and
     plays static until you add a channel.

  2. Press Escape in the video window to open the channel menu, or open
     http://localhost:4242 in a browser for the full console. Add a channel
     by pointing it at a folder of video files.

  3. Rebuild the catalog and add a week of schedule - both are in the menu.

WHERE THINGS LIVE

  Your channel configs, catalog and schedules are in
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\FieldStation42 (Start Menu > FieldStation42 data folder).
  Your video files stay wherever they already are.

PHONE REMOTE

  With the firewall rule enabled, open http://<this computer's name>:4242/remote
  on a phone connected to the same Wi-Fi.

IF SOMETHING IS WRONG

  Start Menu > Check this computer (doctor) writes a report of what works on
  this machine and opens it. Attach it to any bug report.
